Evidence services and API access

Find a service, inspect its current terms and verify the evidence you receive.

Council of AI publishes measurement records and evidence interfaces. Start with the buyer guide, the x402 discovery manifest and the API specification.

Before authorising a purchase

  1. Choose the documented endpoint and read its scope, required inputs and expected output.
  2. Inspect its current response. Where payment is required, use the returned HTTP 402 challenge for the amount, network, asset and recipient.
  3. Authorise only the terms you intend to accept. Retain the request, response and receipt so the transaction and evidence can be checked.
  4. Verify the returned record against the relevant published keys, root and inclusion data. Read any limitations and unmeasured fields.

Availability and payment terms can change. Historical endpoint counts and promotional prices are not current quotes.

What the service provides

Read the selected service's documented deliverable before purchase. Measurement evidence describes an observation within its stated scope. It is not accreditation, a conformity assessment or a guarantee of safety. Payment does not establish the result of a measurement.
